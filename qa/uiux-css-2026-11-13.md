## Per-item verdicts (measured)

### Typography / hierarchy
- **H1:** 24px/30 @320 · 34px/42.5 @390 · 44px/55 @1440 — white `rgb(255,255,255)`,
  1 per page, all 31 routes. Scale rhythm clean except D-1 at 320.
- **H2:** 28px/35 @320/390 · 32px/40 @1440 — pine `rgb(14,59,56)`, 406 total
  (13/page avg). One white H2 per site: `Water Damage Help in Midvale Starts With…`
  (dark section, white on dark — passes).
- **H3:** 24px/30 on all widths, teal `rgb(21,82,76)`, 259 total. H3 (24px) = H1
  size at 320 — same inversion family as D-1, same cause.
- **Body copy:** 1,707 `p`/`li` in `<main>` at **19px** on all three widths.
  Line-height 32.3px (ratio 1.70) at 19px. No real copy below 19px anywhere.
- **Below-19px text in main (chrome only, confirmed unchanged from Stage 0):**
  eyebrow 14px @320 / 18px @390/1440 (31 pages, `WATER DAMAGE RESTORATION •
  MIDVALE, UTAH`); breadcrumb items 18px (63 items). Brand/nav/header-phone
  18px. All chrome, not copy — not re-flagged.
- **Section spacing:** @1440 sections 64px/64px (12), hero 72px/64px;
  @390 sections 40px/40px, hero 48px/56px; @320 hero 16px/40px, sections
  40px/40px. Consistent rhythm, no collapsed or doubled gaps.
- **Alignment:** left-aligned body, centred hero buttons; screenshots confirm
  clean alignment at 320/390/1440 (see PNGs).

### Contrast (AA 4.5:1 text / 3:1 large — real pairs, worst first)
Full sweep across all 31 routes × 3 widths: **0 pairs below AA** (threshold
min + 0.5 margin). Worst pairs anywhere (4-page deep sweep @1440):
- **5.65:1** — white on copper-dark `rgb(157,84,24)` (`#9d5418`): header phone
  button, hero Button 1, sticky-bar link; and the reverse, copper `#9d5418` on
  white: brand city line `Midvale, UT` (18px/700). AA pass, worst on site.
- **8.96:1** — H3 teal `rgb(21,82,76)` on white (card titles, 24px/700).
- H2 pine on white ≈ 10:1; footer links `#dfeae7` on pine ≈ 11:1;
  white H1 on hero photo ≈ 12.34:1 (Button 2 white bg measured 12.34:1).
- **No contrast defect.**

### Header
Sticky (`position:sticky`, white bg), 73px @1440, 107px @320 (two-row: brand +
hamburger; phone hidden ≤360px). Brand `Water Damage Restoration / Midvale, UT`
(18px, pine + copper), nav `Home · Emergency Water Extraction · Sewage Backup
Cleanup` @1440. No overflow, no overlap with hero.

### Hamburger menu (mobile)
Button 46×46, `aria-label="Menu"`, `aria-expanded` toggles. Found + visible on
31/31 @320/@390; hidden @1440 (0×0, `visible:false`). Click verified on `/`,
`/emergency-water-extraction`, `/contact`: panel opens below the sticky header
with the full nav (31 links incl. services, locations, legal, resources, call
link). Closes on second click. Menu links `padding:14px 0` (≈54px rows).

### Footer
27 links on all 31 routes. 4 groups, headings 18px/800 white
(`Services`, `Nearby Cities We Serve`, `About & Resources`, `Emergency Help`);
links 19px `#dfeae7` on pine, 26px row height; grid 5 cols @1440 → 2 cols
≤900px → 1 col ≤600px; padding 56px/36px; `.footer-base` 20px/32px.
Map iframe present 28/31 (320px tall; 3 legal pages have none). **Known open
item, NOT counted:** the Maps embed fails in this VM
(ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED — no route to google.com); 0 local assets fail.

### Sticky call bar
Hidden at first paint **by design** (`.bar-hidden` while the hero is on screen,
toggled by IntersectionObserver). After scrolling past the hero @390 it appears:
62.6px tall, `Call (385) 337-3560 · 24/7`, copper bg. `body{padding-bottom:63px}`
matches the 62.6px bar; `.footer-base` has 76px bottom padding — at page bottom
the bar sits over empty padding, **covers no content**. Desktop: `display:none`.

### Contact form (`/contact` only, by design — renders after the doc's
`[Send My Request]` paragraph)
Fields: Name (text, required), Phone (tel, required), Address or ZIP (text),
What happened (textarea, 122.9px), water-still-coming-in radios Yes / No /
Not sure, Best time to call (text). Submit `[Send My Request]` 231×56.
All text inputs 58.3px tall, 19px font; labels present for every field;
radios are native 13px circles with text labels. No form on home/service pages
(template decision, not a defect).

### Cards
Home service cards: **equal heights within every row @1440**
(545.5×3, 513.1×3, 483.1×3, 513.2×3, 513.1×3, 445.1×3 location cards).
Images `object-fit:cover` 177/177, no distortion. See D-2 for the single
last-row orphan. @320 single column, heights vary by content (expected).

### Tables
129 tables across the site, **0 overflow** at any width. Home table is 392.1px
wide at a 320px viewport but sits inside `.table-wrap{overflow-x:auto}` —
document overflow stays 0; the table scrolls inside its wrapper. Readable.

### FAQ
7 items on home and service pages; H3 questions, answers always visible
(see D-3 — no accordion behaviour). Breadcrumb: present on inner pages
(`Home / <page>`), 18px, truncated to one line with ellipsis @≤360px
(`Home / Rapid Emergency Wat…`); H1 still carries the full title.

### Trust bands
Present on home (`24/7 Emergency Line · Local to Midvale 84047 · Clean, Gray &
Sewage Water · Insurance-Ready Photo Logs`) and service pages (per-service
variant, e.g. `24/7 Pump-Out · Basements to Upper Floors…`). Gold text on
dark hero, readable.

### Tap targets (44px mobile)
1,542 sub-44px instances counted — **all are inline text links** (footer/body
links, 26px tall, 19px font), which meet the WCAG 2.2 minimum of 24×24.
Every real control passes 44px: hamburger 46×46, header phone 46.6px,
hero buttons 48–56px, form inputs 58.3px, submit 56px, menu rows ≈54px.
**No tap-target defect.**

### Horizontal overflow
**0 on 31/31 routes at 320, 390 and 1440** (`scrollWidth − innerWidth = 0`).

### Images
0 broken. Two measurement artefacts, owned plainly: (1) the first pass flagged
many card images "broken" because it never scrolled — they are `loading="lazy"`
below the fold; (2) a fast scroll loop still flagged 5
(`storm-flood-damage`, `mold-remediation`, `cottonwood-heights-ut`,
`south-salt-lake-ut`, `magna-ut`) as unloaded. All 5 files exist in
`out/images/cards/` and return **HTTP 200 with correct byte sizes** via curl —
they simply had not finished lazy-loading in the 120ms/step scroll. The
"distorted" flags were `object-fit:cover` crops (hero 390×1072 from 1600×1066),
not distortion. **0 real image defects.**
