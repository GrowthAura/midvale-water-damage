What causes most water damage in Midvale, UT homes?

Most Midvale water damage comes from frozen or burst pipes, worn water heaters, older sewer lines backing up, and spring snowmelt pushing water through basement walls and window wells.

How Our Water Mitigation Midvale 84047 Process Works

Every job follows the same order, built on IICRC S500 water drying steps, so nothing is missed and your insurer gets a clear record.

  1. Stop the source. We shut off the water, isolate power in wet rooms and make the space safe.
  2. Inspect and map. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show how far the water traveled, including inside walls and under floors.
  3. Extract. Truck-mounted and submersible equipment removes standing water from carpet, pad and hard floors.
  4. Remove what can't be saved. Wet pad, swollen baseboards or contaminated drywall come out, and the rest stays.
  5. Dry and dehumidify. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until readings match dry areas of the home, checked every day.
  6. Clean, then rebuild. Surfaces are cleaned and treated, then drywall, flooring and trim go back in.

From the first phone call to the final walk-through, water mitigation Midvale 84047 homeowners receive is documented with dated photos and daily moisture readings.

Can wet carpet and pad be saved after a leak?

Can mold grow in Midvale when Utah air is so dry?

Yes. Outdoor air is dry, but a wet wall cavity or carpet pad stays damp inside, and mold can start growing on wet drywall within 24 to 48 hours.

Who do I call first for a sewer backup in Midvale?
