Who We Are

We're a water damage company in Midvale, Utah focused on one thing: getting water out of homes and businesses and putting them back the way they were. Our work covers the full range of water problems Midvale properties face:

  • Burst and frozen pipes in older homes and new townhomes.
  • Basement floods from groundwater, window wells and water heaters.
  • Sewage backups through floor drains, toilets and tubs.
  • Swamp cooler, ice dam and bathroom leaks through ceilings.
  • Unit-to-unit leaks in condos, townhomes and apartments.
  • Storm runoff into garages, stairwells and lower levels.
  • Mold that follows slow or hidden leaks.
  • Repairs to drywall, flooring, cabinets and trim after everything is dry.

Our team follows IICRC S500 water drying steps and the IICRC S520 approach for mold, because those are the standards the industry uses to decide when a home is truly dry and clean.

How We Work

  1. We answer, day or night. Your call is answered any hour, and a crew is dispatched immediately.
  2. We make it safe. Water source off, power isolated where needed, hazards marked.
  3. We map the water. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show exactly where the water went.
  4. We remove what can't be saved, and only that. Drying in place comes first whenever it makes sense.
  5. We dry and measure. Daily moisture readings tell us when the job is done, not a guess.
  6. We document everything. Dated photos, readings and lists of removed materials for you and your insurer.
  7. We rebuild. Drywall, texture, paint, flooring and trim, matched to the rest of your home.
