What should I do while waiting for a water damage crew in Midvale?

Shut off the main water valve, cut power to wet rooms if it is safe, lift valuables off wet floors, photograph the damage, and keep everyone away from sewage water.

Those few steps limit how far the water spreads and protect your claim. The main shut-off is usually in the basement or utility room where the water line enters the house. Turn off power at the breaker only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Move rugs, electronics, photos and papers to a dry room. Take photos and video of every wet area before anything is moved. If sewage is coming up a drain, stop using all water in the house and keep children and pets away.

Who to Call First for Utility Problems

Some emergencies start with the city or a sewer district:

  • Sewer backup: stop using water and call your sewer provider to check the main line. Midvalley Sewer District serves much of Midvale at 801-255-7321.
  • Water main break or leak at the meter: Midvale City Public Works at 801-567-7235.
  • Flooding that threatens life, power lines in water or a gas smell: call 911.
