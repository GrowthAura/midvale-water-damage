Why is my swamp cooler leaking through the ceiling in Midvale?

A stuck float valve, clogged drain pan, cracked reservoir, or a cooler that froze before winterizing can overflow onto the roof deck and soak attic insulation and ceiling drywall.

Evaporative coolers work well in Utah's dry summers, which is why so many Midvale roofs have one. But they run on a constant water supply. Fresh water fills a reservoir at the bottom of the cooler, a pump lifts it over the pads, and a float valve shuts the water off when the reservoir is full. When any part of that system fails, water overflows onto the roof or down the duct into the house. Because the cooler sits above the attic, water soaks insulation and the top of the drywall first, and you only see it when the ceiling below can't hold any more.

A float valve stuck open is the most common cause. Hard water builds scale on the float arm and valve seat until it can't close. Within a day, the cooler can send a large amount of water across the roof and into the attic.

Evaporative Cooler Leak Midvale: The Signs Before the Ceiling Fails

An evaporative cooler leak Midvale homes get usually gives warnings first:

  • Water stains or rings around the ceiling vent where cool air comes in.
  • Bubbling or peeling paint on the ceiling.
  • A musty smell when the cooler runs.
  • Water dripping from the vent grille.
  • Soft or sagging drywall in the hallway or room under the cooler.
  • Water running down the outside walls or gutters on dry days.

If you see any of these, shut the cooler off and close its water valve right away.

When a Cooler Freezes Before Winterizing

Midvale's first hard freeze often arrives in October. If the water line isn't shut off and the reservoir isn't drained before then, water inside the line or pan freezes, expands and cracks it. The leak starts on the next warm afternoon, and because the cooler isn't running, nobody is watching. This is one of the most common swamp cooler roof leak calls we see in fall and early winter.

Why Wet Attic Insulation Has to Come Out

Blown-in cellulose and fiberglass soak up water like a sponge. Wet insulation is heavy, and its weight can bring down a ceiling that is already soft. It also packs down and loses most of its insulating value, and it holds moisture against the drywall and framing for weeks. Wet blown-in insulation is usually removed with a vacuum system, the area is dried, and new insulation is added once readings confirm everything is dry.

Side-Mount and Window Coolers

Not every Midvale cooler sits on the roof. Side-mounted and window units leak differently. Overflow runs down the outside wall, soaks the siding and sill plate, and can collect in a window well or along the foundation. Inside, water comes through the window frame or the wall opening and runs down to the floor. These leaks often show up as a wet carpet edge under the window or a basement stain directly below the cooler. The same drying process applies, but the focus moves from the attic to the wall cavity and floor below.

What Adjusters Ask About Cooler Leaks

Insurance adjusters usually want to know when the leak started, whether the cooler was maintained, and whether the damage was sudden. Dated photos of the cooler, the attic and the ceiling, along with daily moisture readings, help answer those questions. Keep any receipts for spring start-up or fall winterizing service; they show the cooler was cared for.

Swamp Cooler Leak FAQ

Should I keep running my swamp cooler if it's leaking?

No. Turn it off and shut its water valve. Running it keeps sending water into the attic.

Will insurance cover swamp cooler water damage?

Sudden overflow damage is often covered. Damage from a long, slow leak or skipped maintenance may not be. Our photos and moisture logs help your adjuster decide.

Can I just paint over a swamp cooler stain?

Not until the area behind it is dry. Painting over wet drywall or insulation traps moisture and lets mold grow out of sight.

How long does attic and ceiling drying take?

Usually about 3 to 5 days, longer if the roof deck or joists were soaked.

When should I winterize my swamp cooler in Midvale?

Before the first hard freeze, usually by early to mid-October.
