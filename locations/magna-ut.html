Why do older Magna homes near Main Street get pipe and sewer leaks?

Many homes near Magna's historic Main Street are decades old, with aging supply pipes and clay sewer lines that crack, fill with roots or shift, then leak into basements.

Homes near Magna's Main Street and the surrounding older neighborhoods were built long before modern plumbing materials. Galvanized steel supply lines rust from the inside, so their walls thin and their joints weaken. Clay and cast-iron sewer lines crack as the ground shifts, and tree roots grow into the joints until the line backs up. Many of these homes have partial basements, crawl spaces or additions built over shallow foundations, so water from a slow leak can collect underneath for weeks. When something finally fails, it usually fails in the lowest, least visible part of the house.

Basement Mold After Flood in Older Homes

Older Magna homes often have unfinished or partly finished basements with block or rubble walls and little ventilation. After a flood, those walls and any paneling or drywall over them hold moisture for a long time. Basement mold after flood is common when drying is left to fans and open windows. Proper drying with dehumidifiers and daily moisture readings, plus removing soaked materials, keeps mold from starting. If you already smell mustiness, the area should be checked behind paneling, under carpet and in corners.

Why Drying Matters in Magna's Cold Months

The west side of the valley can stay cold for long stretches in winter. Cold basements and crawl spaces slow natural evaporation, so wet carpet pad, drywall and wood can stay damp for weeks without equipment. That's enough time for mold to start and for wood floors and subfloors to warp. Dehumidifiers and air movers, along with daily moisture readings, keep drying on schedule and show when the job is truly finished.

Magna Water Damage FAQ

Why do sewer lines back up in older Magna homes?

Clay and cast-iron lines crack and collect tree roots over the years, which narrows the pipe until it blocks.

How do I protect pipes in a Magna crawl space?

Insulate exposed pipes, close crawl space vents in winter and keep the house heated during cold snaps.

Does a crew serve 84044 at night?

Yes. Calls are answered 24/7 and a crew is dispatched immediately.

Will insurance cover my older home's water damage?

Sudden events like burst pipes are usually covered. Slow leaks, wear and groundwater often are not.

Can old plaster walls be dried instead of replaced?

Often, yes, if drying starts quickly. Plaster that separates from the lath may need repair.
